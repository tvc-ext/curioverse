Parent And Safety Notes

Built around privacy, simplicity, and supervision.

Nickname-only identity

Children choose an explorer avatar and broad age band. The current app does not ask for a real name, exact age, school, phone number, email, or location.

Local-first progress

Explorer profile, energy, completed topics, question history, and cached learning content are stored on the device using local app storage.

No ads or trackers

The current app and this website are built without ads, analytics, cookies, trackers, forms, purchases, or backend accounts.

Picture scanner

The scanner can use camera or gallery images. Current image labeling runs on the device; detected label text may be used to request a public fact summary.
